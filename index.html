How it works

From spark to launch.

  1. 1 Discover

    A conversation about your business, your audience, and what makes you different. No jargon required.

  2. 2 Design

    Concepts and drafts you can react to. We iterate together until it clicks.

  3. 3 Refine

    Files, formats, and standards documented — so everything is usable, consistent, and yours.

  4. 4 Launch

    Website live, marketing ready, brand kit delivered. You show up looking professional on day one.

The maker

Hi, I’m Taylor.

I run Muse Makers — a creative hub for businesses at the beginning of their story. I hold an associate’s degree in Graphic Communications from the Community College of Allegheny County and I’m completing my bachelor’s in Media Communications.

From brand standards for a bank to campaigns for nonprofits, packaging, type design, photography, and painting, I bring one consistent creative eye to everything a new business needs. One point of contact, one vision, and work that looks like it costs more than it did.
